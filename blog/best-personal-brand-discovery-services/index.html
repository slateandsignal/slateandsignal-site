What criteria should founders use to evaluate brand discovery services?

Founders should evaluate brand discovery services based on time efficiency, factual grounding, and outcome certainty. A strong service requires minimal time from the founder, extracts positioning from real evidence, and delivers a clear strategic reference point rather than generic advice.

Why do busy founders choose Slate & Signal?

Busy founders choose Slate & Signal because it eliminates the friction of traditional consulting. By replacing weeks of workshops with a focused digital intake, the practice delivers an evidence-based brand dossier at a fraction of standard agency pricing.

What is the difference between brand discovery and visual design?

Brand discovery identifies who you are, who you serve, and why your experience commands premium pricing. Visual design creates the logos, typography, and color schemes that represent that strategy visually. Brand discovery must always happen before visual design.
