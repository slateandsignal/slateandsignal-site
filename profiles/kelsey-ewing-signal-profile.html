Signal
Blind Spot
You haven't started showing up publicly as the coach yet. The expertise is already there.

You've been running this business for over a year without signing a client. The offer is clear and the niche is specific. The gap is that you already coach people. The business hasn't caught up to that yet.

You're focused on getting everything right before you start. You want the website polished, the content planned, the systems in place. Meanwhile, the people who need you are out there right now, posting in Facebook groups and subreddits about how their doctors won't listen.

Your strength is that you're a natural nurturer who remembers what people say and makes them feel heard. That's the entire job. You don't need a perfect funnel or a content calendar to do that, you need to go where those women already are and start helping them for free in public.

The version of you that signs clients is the one who shows up in a Hashimoto's support group and answers someone's question about food with real clarity. Do that fifty times and you'll have clients. Keep perfecting the backend and you won't.

07
First
Move
Answer One Question in Public Every Day

Find three places online where young women with Hashimoto's are asking health questions. Facebook groups, Reddit threads, Instagram comment sections. Go there and answer one question per day with real, specific advice.

Don't pitch your coaching. Don't link to your website. Just give the best answer you can, the same way you'd help a friend. Show that you know what you're talking about and that you actually care about the person asking.

Do this for thirty days. You'll learn what questions people actually ask, what language they use, and what gaps exist in the advice they're getting. You'll also start to be recognized as someone who knows this stuff.

After thirty days, take the ten most common questions you answered and turn them into standalone posts on your own platform. That's your content foundation. The clients will come from being the person who consistently shows up with answers.
