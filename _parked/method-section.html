The Method

Three movements.

01

You answer.

A focused questionnaire draws out the strengths, stories, experiences, and patterns you already carry. Most people find the questions themselves clarifying.

02

We research.

Live market research, niche gap analysis, and verified income comparables. Every claim in your report is sourced. Nothing is invented, estimated, or padded.

03

You receive the map.

A designed report that names your signal, shows the market waiting for it, and gives you the direction to build in. Yours to keep and to build on.
