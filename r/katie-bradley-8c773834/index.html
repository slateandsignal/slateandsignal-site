What this does not decide

This assessment can help you identify the way you naturally communicate and narrow the kinds of content you may want to try.

It does not determine your niche. It does not tell you what makes your particular combination of experience, interests, strengths, and ideas valuable. It does not research your market, competition, opportunities, or positioning.
