06
Signal
Blind Spot
You are waiting for permission to be the expert you already are

You've been trained as a functional medicine health coach. You've done the work to heal yourself. You're the person friends already come to for health advice. You know what you're talking about. But you haven't signed a client yet, and the reason is probably not that your offer is wrong or that you're not qualified.

The reason is that you're not showing up consistently as the expert your ideal client is searching for. You mentioned frustration with relying only on social media to get clients, which tells me you're probably posting sporadically or without a clear strategy. You're waiting for something to click, for someone to give you permission, or for the perfect post to go viral. None of that is going to happen.

What will happen is this: you start posting consistently, three to five times a week, using the three pillars above. You share what you know about food as medicine, about taking health into your own hands, and about the healing journey. You do that for three months without worrying about whether it's perfect or whether anyone is listening. You build a body of work that shows people you know what you're talking about.

The gap is your willingness to be visible before you feel ready. Your dad's advice was to do it right. Your reframe is to not be afraid to make mistakes. Apply that reframe to your content. Post imperfectly. Post consistently. Let people see you as the guide you already are.

07
First
Move
Build a visible body of work before you build an audience

Your first move is not to get more clients. Your first move is to create a content library that proves you know what you're talking about. Pick one of the three pillars above and write ten posts for it. Actually write them, don't just brainstorm ideas. Each post should teach one thing, share one perspective, or answer one question your ideal client is asking.

Once you have ten posts written, start posting them. Three times a week minimum. Do not wait until you have an audience. Do not wait until the posts feel perfect. Post them, let them sit, and keep going. The goal is consistency. You're building a body of work that shows people you are the person who can help them.

While you're posting, pay attention to which posts get responses, which ones people save, and which ones lead to DMs. Those are your signals. Double down on the topics that resonate. Ignore the ones that don't. After three months of consistent posting, you'll have enough data to know what's working and enough visibility to start converting followers into clients.

The second move, once you have momentum, is to add a lead magnet. A free guide, a workshop, a challenge. Something that lets people raise their hand and say they want more from you. But you can't skip the first move. You have to build the visible body of work first. That's what makes the lead magnet convert.
