Blind Spot
You are waiting to feel ready instead of letting the work make you ready

It's because you're treating content and visibility as something you'll do once you feel more prepared, rather than the thing that prepares you.

Your Q10 answer lists writing or talking to yourself, then showing things to your husband or close friends first. That's a safe loop. It keeps the work private. It doesn't put you in front of the women you want to serve. You can research functional medicine for another year and it won't sign a client. Posting consistently will.

You mentioned your dad's advice about doing things right and how you've reframed it as not being afraid to make mistakes. You're still holding back from posting or putting an offer out there because it might not be perfect. The women you want to help don't need you to be perfect. They need you to show up and prove you understand what they're going through.

The other part of this is that you're frustrated by advice that says to rely only on social media for clients, but you haven't clarified what you're doing instead. If social media isn't the main channel, what is? Email? Referrals? Partnerships? You need to pick a channel and commit to showing up there regularly.

07
First
Move
Post three times a week for eight weeks on one platform

Pick Instagram, LinkedIn, or a newsletter platform. Commit to posting three times a week for eight weeks. That's 24 posts. Each one should teach something small and specific about Hashimoto's, functional nutrition, or taking your health into your own hands. Write like you're talking to one woman who just got dismissed by her doctor and has no idea what to do next.

At the end of every post, include one sentence about your coaching offer. Keep it simple. Something like, I work one on one with women navigating Hashimoto's, link in bio if you want to talk. You don't need a perfect website or a polished funnel. You need people to know you exist and that you can help them.

Track how many people reach out. Track which posts get the most response. Adjust based on what you learn. The goal is to prove to yourself that you can show up consistently and that people respond when you do.

After eight weeks, if you've posted consistently and still haven't signed a client, the problem is likely your offer or your pricing. At that point you can adjust the package or how you're positioning it. But you can't adjust an offer nobody knows exists. Post first. Refine second.

Photography Style
Bright, soft, naturally lit
Photos should feel light and airy. Natural light whenever possible. Soft focus is fine. Your tone should sound like someone who listens well and remembers what people tell them. You're walking alongside someone and explaining what you've learned in language they can actually use. Warm but not overly casual. Clear but not clinical. You can be direct about what's not working in traditional medicine without being bitter or preachy. The goal is for someone to read your content and feel like you get it, like you've been exactly where they are and made it through to the other side.
