Blocker: Facebook is a personal profile, not a Page

Personal profiles can't run ads, can't connect to Meta Business Suite, can't access real analytics beyond basic counts, and can't be considered for Meta's Facebook Content Monetization program, which now merges the old In-Stream Ads, Ads on Reels, and Performance Bonus into one system covering Reels, long-form video, photos, and text posts. Converting is a five-minute task and it's the blocker sitting in front of almost everything else on Facebook. Do it before building the media kit, not after.

Real, sourced tool for cutting production time

Speed Up Daily-Tip Production

  • Batch scripting: write every script or prompt for a week's worth of tips in one sitting. AI tools can shift tone or behavior between sessions, so writing in one block keeps the voice consistent across the batch.
  • CapCut's Smart Cut: AI-assisted editing feature reported to cut editing time significantly versus manual cutting. Built-in auto-captions handle the on-screen text pass automatically.
  • Teleprompter note: CapCut's teleprompter is mobile-only, not available on desktop. Plan the recording workflow around that if scripting on a laptop and filming on a phone.
  • Batch filming: record a week of daily tips back to back in one outfit/setting session, then schedule the release through Blotato across the week. Cuts setup and teardown time to once instead of daily.
Embedded in everything, not a separate task

SEO / GEO Fundamentals

SEO (search engines): every title and description gets a real keyword pass, not just a catchy phrase. Consistent naming across every platform: always "Damon Kenrick," same spelling and handle everywhere.

GEO (AI answer engines): Google's own current position is that optimizing for AI Overviews is still SEO, layered on top of the same crawlability and authority signals, not a separate discipline. AI Overviews now appear on a large share of Google searches, and tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity pull and cite sources that write clear, structured, self-contained answers near the top of the page or description. Perplexity specifically rewards recency.

Practical rule: write the first two to three sentences of every description or caption as a self-contained, quotable answer to one specific question, not a teaser. "This shows how to turn a messy meeting into a task list in under two minutes using [tool]" beats a vague hook, because it's the sentence an AI answer engine can lift and cite directly.

Per-platform specifics

YouTube SEO Checklist

  • Title leads with one target keyword and states the outcome clearly. No keyword stuffing.
  • Description opens with the primary keyword in the first two sentences, then 200-300 words of real explanation, not a tag dump.
  • 8-12 tags, mixing broad and niche terms. Tags matter far less than titles and descriptions now, so don't over-invest here.
  • Chapters on every video over five to ten minutes, with descriptive names instead of "Part 1." Chapters are a real ranking factor and individual chapter names can surface directly in search.
  • Watch-through rate is the top ranking signal in 2026, ahead of session time, freshness, and satisfaction. Editing for retention matters more than any metadata trick.
The compounding-content strategy

Pillar Pages: One Per Engine

A pillar page is one comprehensive page that ranks for a broad topic, linked to eight to twelve focused cluster posts underneath it. Together they form a topical authority hub, and AI answer engines specifically reward that kind of whole-topic mastery over a single, narrow ranked page. Sweet spot length is around 3,000 words for the pillar itself.

Pillar 1

AI Tools For Work

One page covering the full landscape of using AI for meeting recaps, task ownership, and time tracking. Cluster posts underneath: one per specific tool or technique, in English first, then Spanish and Portuguese versions once the English pillar proves out.

Pillar 2

Behind Roxie Moonlight / AI Filmmaking

One page covering the full process of building an AI-generated film start to finish. Cluster posts underneath: one per production technique (character consistency, voice work, editing pass, story structure).

Build sequence: pick the pillar topic, map the cluster topics, build the pillar page, then publish supporting cluster content over three to four months. Don't try to write both pillars and all clusters at once. Keep the two pillars on separate pages entirely; mixing AI-tips and AI-film content on one page works against the single-topic depth that AI answer engines reward.

Why This Matters For Content Ideas, Not Just Networking

The goal of joining these communities isn't just visibility. It's watching what questions people actually ask, then answering them in a video.
