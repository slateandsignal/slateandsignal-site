06
Signal
Blind Spot
You are treating all of your interests as equal when one of them is actually the anchor

Your answers list nursing, art, faith, sustainable building, preparedness, crypto, and family as things you care about and could talk about. That is a lot of threads. The risk is that you try to post about all of them at once and end up with a brand that feels scattered, where no one knows what they are following you for.

Your Q8B answer is the clue: life of an artist who happens to be a nurse. That framing makes art the main thing and nursing the context. If that is true, then art is the anchor and everything else either supports it or lives somewhere else entirely.

The same logic applies to the retreat space. If that is the real goal, then content about the space, the work you are making for it, and the vision behind it should be the majority of what you post. Nursing, faith, and preparedness can show up as supporting context when they are relevant, but they should not be competing for equal time with the main thread.

The fix is to choose one anchor and let everything else be secondary. If art and the sanctuary space are the center, lead with that. Post the work, talk about the vision, and let nursing and faith show up when they add something to the story. If nursing is actually the anchor, then flip it and let art be the thing that makes you different from every other nurse online. Either way works, but you have to pick one and commit to it as the main thread.

07
First
Move
Start posting finished paintings with your voice

You said you have a product to sell, which means you already have work that is ready to go. Your first move is to start posting that work with short videos where you talk about it in your own voice. No process footage, no explanation of technique, just you showing the finished piece and saying something true about it.

Pick a platform where short vertical video works well, Instagram Reels or TikTok, and commit to posting one or two of these per week for the next month. Each post should show a painting, include a short caption or voiceover about what you were thinking or feeling when you made it, and either link to where people can buy it or invite them to follow along as you build the sanctuary space.

The goal is to let people see your work and hear your voice so they can decide whether they want more. You are loud, you like talking, and you already know what you want to build. Use that energy and start showing people what you are making right now.

As you post, pay attention to which pieces get the most response and which parts of your story people ask about. That feedback will tell you whether to lean more into the art itself, the sanctuary vision, or the nurse-who-paints angle. You do not have to decide everything up front. Start with the work and let the direction clarify as you go.
