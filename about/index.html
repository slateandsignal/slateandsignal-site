About

A personal brand should begin with the person.

Slate & Signal is a personal brand research practice founded by Raquel Covey. We deliver individualized brand and direction for founders and creators starting from the beginning, rebranding, or bringing an existing brand together.

Why Raquel built Slate & Signal

When Raquel began working in social media management, she noticed a recurring problem. Businesses had a name, a logo, and fonts, but the deeper brand decisions were still unanswered.

What did they want to be known for? What was their voice? What did they want people to understand about them? How should their work feel to someone encountering it for the first time?

Raquel was responsible for creating content that represented the brand, yet often had very little direction to work from. When content felt out of step, there was no clear reference point for explaining what would make it fit.

She saw how difficult marketing becomes when everyone is working from a different understanding of the business. Before the content could communicate the brand, the brand itself needed to be defined.

That became the reason for Slate & Signal.

Raquel developed a method for bringing those unanswered questions together into a complete, individualized brand. Something the founder could recognize as their own, and anyone helping them could understand and work from.

The individual shapes the work

Raquel’s strength is patient, persistent research. She follows connections, examines patterns, and brings scattered information into a form someone can understand and use. She describes it as thinning the weeds to see the garden.

At Slate & Signal, that attention begins with your experiences, strengths, interests, instincts, and perspective. These are the starting materials for a brand crafted around you.

Our method brings together extraction, analysis, research, and curation. We examine what your answers reveal, identify the connections that matter, and develop direction from those findings. For the Signal Report, this includes research into your niche, audience questions, market opportunities, and potential offers.

Each recommendation is individually considered. Each finished brand is curated into a cohesive whole. Your unique qualities shape the result, rather than being fitted into a predetermined brand.

Slate & Signal extracts the raw human elements of a founder and organizes them into a permanent brand architecture.

What you receive

The Signal Profile gives you initial personal brand direction. It identifies your brand archetype, recurring themes, possible niche directions, and guidance for how your brand can look and sound.

The Signal Report delivers your complete personal brand and direction. It brings together positioning, voice, visual identity, niche and topic direction, market research, and monetization opportunities, with cited research supporting the market findings.

You can begin with the Profile or choose the Report directly. Whether you are building your first brand or reconsidering an existing one, the work begins with the individual.

Your finished Report becomes a lasting strategic reference point. You can use it as delivered, adapt parts of it, or share it with the people helping you build.

We deliver the brand foundation and direction. Content production, posting schedules, hooks, and trends are outside the service.
