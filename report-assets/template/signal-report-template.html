HOW TO READ
THIS REPORT

A few terms used throughout, explained plainly before you read the rest.

Signal

The pattern that shows up again and again in what you're drawn to, what people come to you for, and what you've lived through. It was always there. This report exists to name it clearly enough that you can build around it on purpose.

Archetype

The role your signal plays for other people. Not a personality label, a function. It answers the question "what does this person do for the people around them" in one recognizable shape, so an audience can understand you fast.

Constellation

The visual map of your archetype. Each point of light is one real theme from your answers, and the shape they form together is the same shape as your archetype, because the archetype isn't assigned to you, it's built from the actual points. The constellation and the archetype match because one is the picture of the other.

Signal Strength

A 1-10 score for how consistent and specific your signal is right now, not how good or marketable you are. A lower score usually means the signal is real but not yet fully claimed out loud, not that it's weak.

The Niche Gap

Real research into what your specific audience is already asking for online, and where the honest answer is "nobody is fully addressing this yet." This is where you have room to become the clear answer, not just another voice in a crowded topic.

Contrarian Belief

Something you believe, based on real experience, that goes against what most people in your space currently say. These are listed because a contrarian belief stated clearly and often is one of the fastest ways an audience remembers who you are.
